# 🌱 MyGraine

**Understand your migraines. Discover your patterns.**

MyGraine is a personal migraine tracking diary designed to help users better understand their migraines by documenting their daily habits, lifestyle and overall well-being.

By keeping track of daily activities, food intake, mood and potential influencing factors, MyGraine aims to help users identify individual patterns and possible migraine triggers.


- **🍎 Food & Drink Tracking**
- **😊 Mood & Well-being**
- **🏃 Physical Activity**
- **🧠 Migraine Tracking**
- **💤 Lifestyle Tracking**
- **📊 Statistics & Insights**
- **📅 Daily Journal**

## 🔍 Finding Potential Triggers

One of the main goals of MyGraine is to help users discover potential connections between their daily habits and migraine attacks.

For example:
- Does a certain food appear more frequently before migraine attacks?
- Is there a connection between sleep and migraine?
- Does increased stress correlate with more attacks?
- Does physical activity have an influence on overall well-being?


*Note: MyGraine is intended as a personal tracking and self-observation tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.*
